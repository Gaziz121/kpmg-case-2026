## Method / limitations

### Decision and stopping rule

- **Decision:** recommend PASS / UWT / PARK / FAIL for only OP-026 through OP-050 before any ranking. These are Codex screening recommendations for human review, not adopted team decisions.
- **Hypothesis / must be true:** an accessible strategic capital role, reachable buyer volume, a specific Kazakhstan mechanism versus the best realistic supply alternative, feasible inputs/qualification, and a plausible return mechanism must coexist.
- **Kill test:** the CSV records each cheapest adverse fact tested first. Current capture, buyer/product mismatch, upstream timing and qualification received priority. Sources were pursued only while they could alter disposition.
- **Time-box:** targeted desk screening, with unequal depth according to the decisive question. The next tests below are proposed additional desk/specification briefs, not completed validation. Each has proposed owner **Codex** and its own 45–60 minute allowance. No further task or outreach has been launched. Supplier, buyer or regulator responses may be a separate dependency; expiry of a desk time-box is not a negative commercial response.
- **Stop rule:** stop on a fatal contradiction if found; otherwise record the decisive uncertainty as UWT or PARK the thesis for a stated current-case reason. The 17 UWTs are worth a bounded test. They are not 17 validated projects or a request to fund deep research automatically.

No PASS is assigned because the remaining questions attack the central capital-access, reachable-scale or location-economics mechanism, rather than peripheral execution details. No FAIL is assigned because the evidence did not establish a fatal contradiction across the defined opportunity's full buyer/asset scope. Existing competitors, announced projects, missing quotations and missing contracts were not treated as proof of universal capture. PARK is an absolute deadline/promotion judgment, not rejection because another OP ranks higher. No fixed minimum CAPEX, market threshold, horizon, numeric attractiveness score, Top 5 or portfolio quota was imposed.

All rows retain the master hypothesis's product/capital scope and proposed Kazakhstan place logic. Research findings do not silently relocate a plant, add new products/markets or turn an unavailable equity role into a different service business. In OP-044, dry fractionation and later wet isolation require separate viability checks inside the recorded phased business; no automatic wet-phase funding is assumed. Integrated package boundaries are preserved for human judgment.

### Evidence and interpretation

**FACT** means a source states the claim, including an explicitly attributed company/operator assertion. It does not certify the assertion independently. **CALCULATION** is reproducible arithmetic. **ASSUMPTION** identifies an input used only for illustration. **INFERENCE** is the causal/economic interpretation. **UNKNOWN** is not established. Status and effort decisions are **JUDGMENT**.

Every material external statement in the CSV resolves to the source keys below and to that row's working URLs. Sources were tool-opened on **23 October 2026 (Asia/Almaty)**; the source date/reporting period is given separately. Undated web pages are not proof of a fresh status update. Human confirmation of decisive source support remains outstanding under the research protocol. No new source-register file or collaborative tracker was changed.

Rivals are screening benchmarks, not certified final strongest-location choices. The precise best quote/plant/route remains unresolved for OP-026–OP-032, OP-034, OP-037, OP-039, OP-041 and OP-043–OP-049. For the PARK rows, an existing domestic converter, financed expansion, qualified import chain or existing logistics service is the relevant adverse alternative, but the globally cheapest supplier is still unproven. Each required next comparison must use the actual buyer/product/route and eligible counterparties. No China/Russia/Uzbekistan label by itself establishes superiority or market access.

National power, gas, water or polymer capacity was not assumed available at an individual site. Qualification and origin rules were not inherited from a different plant/product. There is no DCF, exhaustive customs sweep, plant design, market forecast or finalist comparable/base-rate exercise. Actual design/quote breakeven throughput must supply the thresholds in next-stage tests; no invented utilization hurdle is used.
