## Method / limitations

**Decision and stopping rule.** Determine whether to fund a next validation step for each admitted capital decision. For each row, the original thesis and cheapest kill were considered before demand, location economics, physical feasibility and capture. Must be true across the batch: a real investable capital slot; paying reachable buyers at viable utilisation; a defensible Kazakhstan mechanism against the strongest actual alternative; necessary physical/commercial access; and a plausible margin. Stop on a fatal contradiction or a bounded unresolved test. No new hypothesis, alternative product package, DCF, sector quota or numerical attractiveness score was created.

**Fairness and provenance.** The master/provenance and merge log were read. Only the assigned master rows were screened. The official Aibek source CSV fields were consulted for vague assigned rows; they did not supply missing commercial specificity. No source stream, mention count, card polish or citation count entered the status rule. Sites and process variants that the originals left open remain UNKNOWN or are future test locations, not silently repaired hypotheses. The CSV considers market, scale, utilities, capture and return mechanism even where evidence is incomplete. JUDGMENT and INFERENCE are analytical assessments; UNKNOWN means not established, not negative evidence. Future-test break-even and competitive-cost conditions are tests, not invented numerical forecasts.

**Research access and biological scope.** OP-077 and OP-084 are deliberately UWT — manual review required due to research-access limitation under the user's narrowed instructions. Only non-sensitive business demand/status, existing partnership plans and investor logic are recorded. No biological experiments, vaccine design, pathogen manipulation or laboratory methods/protocols were performed or included. OP-080, OP-095 and OP-096 are also confined to non-sensitive commercial, supply, project-status and access questions; no technical biological validation was attempted. The manual limitation does not imply a fatal investment flaw.
