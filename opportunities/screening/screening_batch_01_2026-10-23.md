## Method / limitations

### Screening standard and boundaries

- Decision: fund a bounded central-hinge test, park for the current deadline, or reject an evidenced broken mechanism. The original master investor/product/place/market scope was retained. No hypothesis was repaired by selecting a new asset or business model.
- Must be true: a real incremental capital action, paying reachable demand, a buyer-valued location factor, accessible inputs/rights and plausible margin. Each row records its cheapest killer first; depth then follows what could change the status. Project status checks distinguish plans/MoUs, signed terms, financing, construction and operation.
- The master universe, assigned provenance records and merge log were read as canonical inputs. Their historical FACT labels were treated as claims to check. Multi-source variants were not counted as independent external evidence. No official source-discovery card was needed beyond the assigned master/provenance because the investor and capital actions were intelligible there.
- FACT means the cited source states it; it is not an audit of an issuer's claim. INFERENCE explains causation; HYPOTHESIS preserves the submitted proposal; UNKNOWN means not established. All statuses and resource-allocation conclusions are JUDGMENT by this screen, not recorded team decisions. No new quantitative operating-cost assumptions, DCF or attractiveness score were introduced.
- Scale is product- and plant-specific; no invented universal strategic-investor minimum was imposed. Capacity, resources, total waste and total battery investment were not treated as reachable sales. The UWT conditions compare customer volume with a vendor-confirmed viable line rather than manufacture a minimum.
- No PASS was assigned, so the full three-perspective NO case, revealed-preference and outside-view validation requirements for serious PASS survivors were not bypassed. Current rival cases are recorded in each row; full comparable-project studies and agency counterfactuals were outside this cheap pass.
