## Method / limitations

**Decision and stopping rule.** Decide only whether the admitted investor-location decision deserves further validation. For each row, the CSV records the original capital package, reachable payer, trigger, candidate/best observed alternative, deciding factor, physical constraints, capture status, return mechanism and cheapest potential killer. The initial potential killer determined targeted searches; no DCF, numeric score, portfolio optimisation or comparative author assessment was performed. The search moved from status/rights to demand and economics only as necessary. No opportunity outside the assigned batch received a screening disposition.

**Fair treatment and thesis boundaries.** Every stream used the same absolute questions and evidentiary burden. Source counts, polishing and amount of initial research confer no benefit. Raw genetics, germanium, casing and seed hypotheses remain testable; detailed announcements do not earn PASS. Parks reflect the current capital proposition and observed opposing mechanism, not lack of convenient citations. For OP-058 and OP-059 the integrated packages and unresolved merge boundaries are preserved. Adjacent OPs referenced by provenance were not screened, merged or repaired. No new product/site/business model was substituted for a failed claim. Site and capital-size gaps remain UNKNOWN where the admitted thesis and verified evidence do not establish them. There is no team-approved monetary scale threshold; viability tests compare actual reachable volumes with supplier/project minimum viable scale rather than inventing one.

**Claim types and confidence.** FACT means directly stated by the linked source, not independently audited truth. CALCULATION is explicitly arithmetic; INFERENCE is a causal interpretation; JUDGMENT is this screening recommendation; UNKNOWN/source-thesis labels distinguish unverified claims. There is no quantitative return forecast or hidden assumed market share. The scenario bounds are the explicit pass/kill conditions, not invented numeric forecasts. Overall confidence is moderate for observed milestones and incumbent capabilities, low for project-specific costs, contracts and remaining market share. Sources were opened by the AI; required human verification before canonical evidence-log/slide use remains outstanding. No human verified_by is claimed.

**Adversarial check.** The recurring CFO objection is that no uncommitted order/offtake covers fixed capital; the rival objection is that existing approved suppliers or brownfield assets already offer the stated proximity/cost benefit; the judge objection is the leap from a national programme/nameplate/endowment to a specific open capital slot. The CSV applies the relevant objection per row rather than offsetting it with generic benefits. None receives PASS, so no unresolved decisive NO case is presented as closed. Two-to-three location-choice comparables, completion base rates and agency counterfactuals remain later validation work, not a fabricated cheap-screen requirement.
