## Cluster A — Acid, sulphur and phosphorus chemistry

### H01 — Merchant sulphuric-acid "utility" for ISR uranium in the south
- **COMBINES:** surplus Tengiz/Kashagan sulphur + world-leading ISR uranium mining + south-Kazakhstan rail.
- **INPUT / EXISTING CAPABILITY:** ≈5.1 Mt/yr of sulphur, almost all exported raw ([SMM](https://news.metal.com/newscontent/103696006)). Kazatomprom is the world's largest uranium producer [verify share]. Its ISR mines consume large volumes of acid.
- **VALUE-CHAIN GAP:** Sulphur leaves the country as a commodity while uranium output has been capped by acid shortages ([NucNet](https://smr.nucnet.org/news/kazatomprom-concludes-loan-deal-in-bid-to-finance-key-sulphuric-acid-plant-5-1-2025)).
- **SPECIFIC INVESTMENT OPPORTUNITY:** An independent acid supplier with sulphur-burning acid plant(s) in Turkestan/Kyzylorda, a rail tank-car fleet and buffer storage. It would sell under 10–15-year take-or-pay contracts to Kazatomprom JVs (Orano, Cameco, CGN, Uranium One partners). Waste heat from sulphur burning would feed steam turbines for on-site power.
- **OUTPUT:** Technical sulphuric acid (~93–98%), plus by-product electricity.
- **BUYER:** ISR uranium JVs. Secondary buyers: phosphate fertiliser producers (Kazphosphate, EuroChem Karatau).
- **REACHABLE MARKET:** Domestic, but with a large, contractable volume. Kazatomprom's real acid consumption needs a T1 figure from its annual report [verify].
- **WHY KAZAKHSTAN:** The demand is site-locked: ISR mines can't move. Acid is costly and hazardous to transport long distances, so it is a natural local monopoly-like utility with feedstock on hand.
- **STRONGEST ALTERNATIVE LOCATION:** Not a location but a rival model: Kazatomprom building its own plants (it already does) and acid from the new copper smelters from ~2030.
- **INVESTOR RETURN LOGIC:** Infrastructure-style returns from contracted volumes. Margin comes from the spread between sulphur netback (low, export-parity) and delivered acid price (high, import-parity in a deficit market). Power by-product adds revenue.
- **MAIN OBSTACLE:** Timing. Planned 800 kt plants plus 1.5 Mt+ of smelter acid could flip the balance into **surplus after ~2030**, stranding a merchant plant. There is also counterparty concentration (one state-linked buyer group).
- **WHY THIS COULD BE NON-OBVIOUS:** It looks like a commodity chemical. The value is actually *security of supply for the world's swing uranium producer*. Western utilities and Kazatomprom's foreign JV partners may value this enough to co-invest.
- **NEXT DECISIVE TEST:** Build a regional acid supply–demand balance for 2026–2034 (south vs east/central, including smelter acid and rail distance). Kill the idea if a structural surplus appears in the south before 2030.
- **SOURCE LINKS:** [NucNet acid plant loan](https://smr.nucnet.org/news/kazatomprom-concludes-loan-deal-in-bid-to-finance-key-sulphuric-acid-plant-5-1-2025) · [SMM Kazakhstan sulphur](https://news.metal.com/newscontent/103696006) · [Balkhash smelter acid](https://news.metal.com/en/newscontent/104036568-kazakhstan-approves-300000-mt-copper-smelting-project-in-balkhash-planned-to-start-production-in-2030)

### H02 — Sulphur → carbon disulphide → xanthate flotation reagents for Central Asian copper and gold
- **COMBINES:** raw sulphur surplus + a fast-growing regional flotation base (KZ copper smelters, Uzbekistan's Almalyk expansion) + central position.
- **INPUT / EXISTING CAPABILITY:** Sulphur (H01). Copper concentrators at Aktogay and Bozshakol feed the new smelters ([Times of CA](https://timesca.com/copper-smelter-to-be-built-in-eastern-kazakhstan/)). Uzbekistan's new Almalyk concentrator handles 60 Mt/yr of ore ([Kun.uz](https://kun.uz/en/69743581)).
- **VALUE-CHAIN GAP:** Flotation collectors (xanthates, dithiophosphates) and frothers are consumables every concentrator buys continuously. In the region they are largely imported, mostly from China [verify with Comtrade HS 2930].
- **SPECIFIC INVESTMENT OPPORTUNITY:** A reagent plant (CS2 + alcohol + caustic → sodium/potassium xanthates, with optional dithiophosphates) in the Pavlodar or Jibek Joly chemical SEZ. Partners would be a global mining-chemicals company (e.g., Orica, Solvay/Syensqo, Clariant mining, Chinese Qixia-type producers), with toll-blending and technical service teams at mines.
- **OUTPUT:** SIBX/PAX xanthates, plus blended collector packages.
- **BUYER:** Copper, gold and polymetallic concentrators in KZ, Uzbekistan, Kyrgyzstan, Tajikistan and Mongolia.
- **REACHABLE MARKET:** Regional. Size needs tonnage of ore floated × dosage (inference: tens of kt/yr of reagents across the region [verify]). It may be **too small for a major alone**, so pair it with H28/H29 as a "mining chemicals park".
- **WHY KAZAKHSTAN:** Feedstock sulphur on site. It is the largest flotation market in the region, and rail to Uzbekistan, Kyrgyzstan and Mongolia lets one plant serve all of them.
- **STRONGEST ALTERNATIVE LOCATION:** China (scale and cost leader; ships by rail through Khorgos).
- **INVESTOR RETURN LOGIC:** Sticky, service-led consumable sales. Reagent suppliers earn on technical lock-in, not commodity price. Lead time and supply security matter to mines after China export uncertainty.
- **MAIN OBSTACLE:** Chinese price competition. CS2 handling is hazardous. The scale may be sub-strategic.
- **WHY THIS COULD BE NON-OBVIOUS:** Nobody thinks of Tengiz sulphur and copper mining as one chain. The by-product of oil feeds a reagent used by metals.
- **NEXT DECISIVE TEST:** Pull HS 2930.90 (xanthates) imports for KZ, Uzbekistan and Kyrgyzstan from Comtrade/ITC. Below ~US$50m/yr combined → merge into a broader mining-chemicals cluster or PARK.
- **SOURCE LINKS:** [SMM sulphur](https://news.metal.com/newscontent/103696006) · [Almalyk concentrator](https://kun.uz/en/69743581) · [KAZ Minerals smelter feed](https://timesca.com/copper-smelter-to-be-built-in-eastern-kazakhstan/)

### H03 — Sulphur-bentonite and sulphur-enhanced fertilisers for export
- **COMBINES:** sulphur surplus + existing phosphate/fertiliser base (Kazphosphate, Karatau) + bentonite [verify local deposits] + rail access to China, South Asia via Iran, and Black Sea markets.
- **INPUT / EXISTING CAPABILITY:** Sulphur is exported raw to Morocco, Israel, Egypt, China, Brazil and others ([SMM](https://news.metal.com/newscontent/103696006)). Kazphosphate made 750 kt of ammophos in 2025 and is planning MAP and sulfoammophos ([Fertilizer Daily](https://www.fertilizerdaily.com/20260211-kazphosphate-reports-record-output-in-2025)).
- **VALUE-CHAIN GAP:** Raw sulphur is a price-taker commodity. Agricultural sulphur products (S-bentonite pastilles, S-coated or S-fortified NPK) sell at a premium per tonne of S [verify premium].
- **SPECIFIC INVESTMENT OPPORTUNITY:** A sulphur-bentonite pastillation plant at the Tengiz/Atyrau end, or co-located with Kazphosphate (Zhambyl) to make S-enriched phosphates. The partner would be a fertiliser trader or producer (e.g., Yara, OCP, Tiger-Sul type specialist).
- **OUTPUT:** 90% S-bentonite pastilles; sulphur-enhanced MAP/NPK.
- **BUYER:** Fertiliser distributors in China, India/Pakistan (via the Iran rail route), Central Asia and Turkey.
- **REACHABLE MARKET:** Export-scale. Sulphur deficiency in soils is a growing agronomic issue in South Asia and China [verify].
- **WHY KAZAKHSTAN:** Free feedstock relative to coastal producers, rail access to inland Asian markets that Gulf sea-borne S-bentonite reaches less cheaply, and phosphate synergy.
- **STRONGEST ALTERNATIVE LOCATION:** UAE/Saudi (ADNOC/Ma'aden sulphur, sea access); Russia (Astrakhan, Orenburg sour gas).
- **INVESTOR RETURN LOGIC:** Upgrading a low-netback product. The premium over raw S pays for a modest capex.
- **MAIN OBSTACLE:** The inland-market logistics advantage must beat sea freight from the Gulf to India. Product registration in each market.
- **WHY THIS COULD BE NON-OBVIOUS:** Sulphur is usually treated as a disposal problem at Tengiz, not as an agri-input.
- **NEXT DECISIVE TEST:** Get the price spread of S-bentonite vs granular sulphur (Argus/CRU/Profercy) and the landed-cost comparison KZ→Xinjiang/India vs Gulf→India.
- **SOURCE LINKS:** [SMM sulphur](https://news.metal.com/newscontent/103696006) · [Kazphosphate 2025 output](https://www.fertilizerdaily.com/20260211-kazphosphate-reports-record-output-in-2025)

### H04 — Battery-grade purified phosphoric acid / iron phosphate (LFP precursor) from Karatau phosphate
- **COMBINES:** Karatau phosphate rock + Kazphosphate's thermal (yellow) phosphorus know-how [verify current P4 operation] + new sulphuric acid capacity + non-China supply-chain rules (US FEOC, EU CRMA).
- **INPUT / EXISTING CAPABILITY:** Kazphosphate mines Karatau rock (≈1.4 Mt 2025), makes acid and fertilisers, and is building an 800 kt acid plant and 1 Mt MAP ([Fertilizer Daily](https://www.fertilizerdaily.com/20260211-kazphosphate-reports-record-output-in-2025)). Government aims to process materials for LFP batteries ([Reuters via Mining.com](https://www.mining.com/web/kazakhstan-seeks-niche-in-battery-supply-chain/)). Company profile: [kpp.kz](https://kpp.kz/en/about).
- **VALUE-CHAIN GAP:** Phosphate goes into low-value fertiliser. Battery-grade purified phosphoric acid (PPA) and FePO4 are dominated by China.
- **SPECIFIC INVESTMENT OPPORTUNITY:** A PPA purification unit (solvent extraction of wet-process acid, or a thermal route) plus an FePO4 precursor line in Jibek Joly / Khimpark SEZ. A Korean, Japanese or Western LFP cathode maker would take equity and offtake.
- **OUTPUT:** Battery-grade H3PO4; ferric phosphate (FePO4) for LFP/LMFP cathodes.
- **BUYER:** Non-Chinese LFP cathode producers (Korea, Japan, EU, US) and Chinese cathode makers with plants outside China.
- **REACHABLE MARKET:** Global, growing with LFP adoption in EVs and storage.
- **WHY KAZAKHSTAN:** One of few non-China places with phosphate rock, phosphorus chemistry experience and sulphur and acid on hand. Plausible "non-FEOC" origin [verify the legal test].
- **STRONGEST ALTERNATIVE LOCATION:** **Morocco** (OCP: phosphate scale, sea access, EU/US-friendly, already moving into LFP materials) [verify].
- **INVESTOR RETURN LOGIC:** Premium pricing of battery-grade over fertiliser-grade phosphate. Offtake secured by a cathode maker that needs compliant origin.
- **MAIN OBSTACLE:** Morocco is the better-positioned rival. Karatau rock impurity profile (Mg, Fe, Al, Cd) and purification cost are unknown. Kazakhstan is landlocked.
- **WHY THIS COULD BE NON-OBVIOUS:** Kazakhstan is seen as a fertiliser exporter, not a battery-chemicals location. The thermal phosphorus heritage is little known.
- **NEXT DECISIVE TEST:** Get a technical datasheet on Karatau rock/acid impurities and the cost curve for PPA purification vs Morocco. Kill the idea if the cost penalty beats the logistics and FEOC premium.
- **SOURCE LINKS:** [Kazphosphate](https://www.fertilizerdaily.com/20260211-kazphosphate-reports-record-output-in-2025) · [Reuters battery niche](https://www.mining.com/web/kazakhstan-seeks-niche-in-battery-supply-chain/) · [kpp.kz](https://kpp.kz/en/about)

### H05 — Scale-up of high-purity manganese sulphate (HPMSM) with a cathode-maker offtake
- **COMBINES:** domestic manganese ore (Qarmet, Zhairem) + sulphuric acid + cheap power + an existing certified HPMSM producer.
- **INPUT / EXISTING CAPABILITY:** TAZA Metal Technologies has made HPMSM (max 22.5 kt/yr) since 2023, certified in 2024, and plans an industrial park in Aksu, Pavlodar ([IMnI](https://www.manganese.org/en/taza-metal-technologies)). Qarmet started manganese mining at Zakhidny Karazhal ([GMK](https://gmk.center/en/news/qarmet-has-commenced-manganese-ore-mining-at-zakhidny-karazhal/)).
- **VALUE-CHAIN GAP:** A small producer, while LMFP and high-Mn chemistries need large volumes. Battery-grade Mn is >90% China-processed [verify].
- **SPECIFIC INVESTMENT OPPORTUNITY:** A strategic equity plus offtake partner (Korean, Japanese or EU cathode/pCAM maker) funds a 50–100 kt/yr expansion in Pavlodar SEZ. Optional add: electrolytic manganese metal.
- **OUTPUT:** HPMSM (battery-grade MnSO4·H2O).
- **BUYER:** NMC/LMFP/LMR pCAM producers outside China.
- **REACHABLE MARKET:** Global battery supply chain. Volumes need [verify] from Benchmark/Project Blue.
- **WHY KAZAKHSTAN:** Integrated ore → product, a working certified operator (lower execution risk than greenfield), acid and power.
- **STRONGEST ALTERNATIVE LOCATION:** China (incumbent). South Africa/Gabon ore with processing in Europe/US (e.g., Element 25 in the US, Euro Manganese in Czechia).
- **INVESTOR RETURN LOGIC:** Ex-China premium plus security of supply. The partner buys into an existing, qualified process instead of a pilot.
- **MAIN OBSTACLE:** HPMSM price volatility and Chinese overcapacity. LMFP adoption pace is uncertain.
- **WHY THIS COULD BE NON-OBVIOUS:** A Kazakh battery-grade producer already exists and is little known outside the industry.
- **NEXT DECISIVE TEST:** Interview TAZA on its expansion appetite, unit costs and certification status. Benchmark against non-China HPMSM project costs.

## Cluster B — Nuclear fuel cycle

### H06 — Western-aligned UF6 conversion at Ulba (Ust-Kamenogorsk)
- **COMBINES:** largest U3O8 output in the world + Ulba's existing uranium-processing, fluorine and HF chemistry + Western need to avoid Russian conversion + Middle Corridor routing.
- **INPUT / EXISTING CAPABILITY:** Kazatomprom U3O8. Ulba already processes uranium and produces **hydrofluoric acid** ([Ulba products](https://ulba.kz/en/tantalum-products), [Ulba natural U3O8](https://ulba.kz/en/natural-u3o8)). A 51/49 Kazatomprom–Cameco "Ulba Conversion" JV for ~12 kt UF6 was studied in 2008–09 and not built ([WNN](https://world-nuclear-news.org/articles/progress-in-kazakh-ambitions)).
- **VALUE-CHAIN GAP:** Kazakhstan exports U3O8 concentrate. Conversion, the next stage, happens in Russia, Canada, France, the US or China.
- **SPECIFIC INVESTMENT OPPORTUNITY:** A revived conversion JV with a Western converter (Cameco, Orano or ConverDyn/Honeywell), sized against Western utility demand to replace Russian conversion. UF6 would be shipped via the Trans-Caspian route.
- **OUTPUT:** Natural UF6.
- **BUYER:** Western (and possibly Asian) utilities and enrichers (Urenco, Orano).
- **REACHABLE MARKET:** Global conversion market. Western capacity is reported tight (ConverDyn Metropolis "sold out through 2028": [Energy Intelligence headline](https://www.energyintel.com/0000018b-d951-dbb5-a5ef-dd7328030000), paywalled; [verify]).
- **WHY KAZAKHSTAN:** Feed is at the mine gate. Ulba has nuclear-materials licensing culture and HF capability. It avoids shipping concentrate halfway around the world before conversion.
- **STRONGEST ALTERNATIVE LOCATION:** Expanding existing Western plants (Port Hope in Canada, Philippe Coste in France, Metropolis in the US). These are politically preferred by utilities.
- **INVESTOR RETURN LOGIC:** Conversion prices rose sharply after 2022 [verify]. Long-term contracts with utilities. Possible Western government or DFC support (the US is already backing Kazakh tungsten: see H08).
- **MAIN OBSTACLE:** Utilities may prefer Western-sited conversion. UF6 transport is more hazardous than U3O8 and routing avoids Russia only via the Caspian. Long licensing. The original 2008 JV was abandoned [find out why].
- **WHY THIS COULD BE NON-OBVIOUS:** It was tried once and forgotten. The post-2022 sanctions environment changes the economics of the old idea.
- **NEXT DECISIVE TEST:** Find why the 2008 Cameco JV stalled, current conversion spot and term prices, and whether Kazatomprom is still pursuing conversion (annual report / CMD). If stalled for structural reasons that still hold → FAIL.
- **SOURCE LINKS:** [WNN 2009 Ulba Conversion JV](https://world-nuclear-news.org/articles/progress-in-kazakh-ambitions) · [Ulba products incl. HF](https://ulba.kz/en/tantalum-products) · [Energy Intel – Metropolis sold out](https://www.energyintel.com/0000018b-d951-dbb5-a5ef-dd7328030000)

## Cluster C — Critical and specialty metals

### H07 — Gallium: refining to 6N+ and "design-in" at the new alumina refineries
- **COMBINES:** Pavlodar Bayer liquor + ERG proprietary recovery + a planned 2 Mt/yr East Hope alumina refinery + China export controls + Japanese/US buyers.
- **INPUT / EXISTING CAPABILITY:** ERG will recover ~15 t/yr gallium at Pavlodar from Q3 2026 under a long-term deal with Mitsubishi RtM Japan ([Astana Times](https://astanatimes.com/2025/12/kazakhstan-leverages-critical-minerals-diplomacy-to-become-no-2-gallium-producer/)). East Hope (China) has committed $12.6bn to a 2 Mt alumina and 1 Mt aluminium complex in Kostanay/Aktobe ([SMM](https://news.metal.com/en/newscontent/103921363-central-asias-aluminum-map-one-integrated-chain-one-smelter-in-search-of-feedstock-and-a-126-billion-chinese-bet)).
- **VALUE-CHAIN GAP:** (a) The output is primary (≈4N) gallium; high-purity 6N–7N refining and compounds (TMGa, GaAs/GaN feedstock) happen elsewhere. (b) The new refinery could recover gallium from day one but has no stated plan.
- **SPECIFIC INVESTMENT OPPORTUNITY:** A Japanese, Korean or US semiconductor-materials firm builds a small 6N refining plant in Pavlodar SEZ fed by ERG. In parallel, it negotiates gallium-circuit design-in at East Hope's refinery with ex-China offtake. Germanium recovery from coal fly ash or zinc residues could be studied as a sister product [verify feasibility].
- **OUTPUT:** 6N/7N gallium; possibly gallium trichloride/TMGa.
- **BUYER:** Compound semiconductor wafer and epitaxy makers (Japan, US, EU, Korea).
- **REACHABLE MARKET:** Global, small by tonnage but strategically priced. China suspended its US ban until 27 Nov 2026, but licensing controls remain ([Engineering News](https://www.engineeringnews.co.za/article/china-halts-ban-on-gallium-germanium-antimony-exports-to-us-but-controls-remain-2025-11-10)).
- **WHY KAZAKHSTAN:** One of very few non-China primary gallium sources with refinery feed already flowing. Government-to-government critical-minerals deals with Japan, the EU and the US.
- **STRONGEST ALTERNATIVE LOCATION:** Japan/Germany/Canada for refining (closer to customers). Primary gallium at other refineries (Germany's restarted capacity, Australia proposals) [verify].
- **INVESTOR RETURN LOGIC:** Refining margin plus security premium. Low capex. Value per kg is high, so air or rail freight is irrelevant to cost.
- **MAIN OBSTACLE:** The tiny market means sub-strategic ticket size for anyone but a niche specialist. China can collapse prices when it lifts controls.
- **WHY THIS COULD BE NON-OBVIOUS:** The obvious play (primary gallium) is already done. The overlooked play is the *next* refinery and the purity step.
- **NEXT DECISIVE TEST:** Ask ERG/Mitsubishi whether refining is planned in Japan. Find whether the East Hope design includes Ga circuits. Get the 4N→6N price spread (Fastmarkets/Argus).
- **SOURCE LINKS:** [Astana Times – ERG gallium](https://astanatimes.com/2025/12/kazakhstan-leverages-critical-minerals-diplomacy-to-become-no-2-gallium-producer/) · [SMM aluminium map](https://news.metal.com/en/newscontent/103921363-central-asias-aluminum-map-one-integrated-chain-one-smelter-in-search-of-feedstock-and-a-126-billion-chinese-bet) · [China controls status](https://www.engineeringnews.co.za/article/china-halts-ban-on-gallium-germanium-antimony-exports-to-us-but-controls-remain-2025-11-10)

### H08 — Tungsten carbide powder and hardmetal tools downstream of Kazakh APT
- **COMBINES:** the world's largest undeveloped tungsten resource + US-financed APT project + a regional mining market that consumes drill bits and wear parts + China tungsten export controls.
- **INPUT / EXISTING CAPABILITY:** Upper Kairakty/Northern Katpar (Cove Kaz Capital 70% / Tau-Ken 30%): ~12 kt/yr WO3 potential (~15% of global mine output). All ore is to be processed domestically to **APT**. DFS runs to end-2027; capex ~$1.1bn, with EXIM and DFC letters of interest. Boguty (Jiaxin) has produced since 2024 ([Astana Times](https://astanatimes.com/?p=118293), [BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260213536464/en/Cove-Kaz-Capital-Group-and-Tau-Ken-Samruk-Execute-Definitive-Agreements-to-Develop-the-Worlds-Largest-Known-Undeveloped-Tungsten-Resource)).
- **VALUE-CHAIN GAP:** The plan stops at APT. The next steps (WO3/blue oxide → W powder → WC powder → cemented carbide inserts and mining buttons) capture most of the value [verify the price ladder].
- **SPECIFIC INVESTMENT OPPORTUNITY:** A carbide-powder plant (hydrogen reduction + carburisation) next to the APT plant in Karaganda / Saryarka SEZ. Partner: a Western/Japanese/Korean hardmetal group (Sandvik, Kennametal, Plansee/Ceratizit, Mitsubishi Materials, Korea Tungsten). Phase 2: mining tool buttons and drill bits for Central Asian mines.
- **OUTPUT:** WC powder, ready-to-press grades; later carbide mining tools.
- **BUYER:** Hardmetal and toolmakers outside China; regional mining companies for finished tools.
- **REACHABLE MARKET:** Global (EU, US, Japan, Korea). China supplies most of the world's tungsten and has tightened export controls ([Project Blue](https://projectblue.com/blue/news-analysis/1297/what’s-behind-the-surge-in-tungsten-prices)).
- **WHY KAZAKHSTAN:** Captive APT feed at scale, US government financing interest, and local hydrogen (natural gas or, later, green). Mining demand at the doorstep for tools.
- **STRONGEST ALTERNATIVE LOCATION:** Vietnam (Masan/H.C. Starck chain); South Korea (Sangdong mine + downstream); processing in Austria/Germany using imported APT.
- **INVESTOR RETURN LOGIC:** Vertical integration margin. Customers pay a premium for non-China, traceable powder. The anchor investor may want to own the downstream piece.
- **MAIN OBSTACLE:** Mine timing (no production before ~2029–30). Powder quality qualification. Cobalt binder sourcing.

- **WHY KAZAKHSTAN:** Low-cost wheat (often discounted in high-crop years), government co-funding, and rail access.
- **STRONGEST ALTERNATIVE LOCATION:** China (corn-based lysine leader); Russia (Exiton, Miratorg amino acid plants) [verify].
- **INVESTOR RETURN LOGIC:** Cheap glucose feedstock; co-product revenue (gluten exports already go to Japan: [KFP headline](https://kfp.kz/en/press-center/biooperations-begins-exporting-wheat-gluten-to-japan), [verify]).
- **MAIN OBSTACLE:** Chinese lysine prices are very low. Wheat starch is costlier to process than corn [verify].
- **WHY THIS COULD BE NON-OBVIOUS:** The state is funding the plants; the investor case is the missing technology layer.

### H32 — Malting plant for Chinese and Central Asian brewers
- **COMBINES:** ~4 Mt barley harvest, 1.8–2 Mt exported + barley exports to China limited by border-station congestion + higher value density of malt + Chinese and regional beer demand.

- **REACHABLE MARKET:** Western China + Central Asia.

- **MAIN OBSTACLE:** Malting-barley quality (varieties, protein); Chinese phytosanitary access for malt [verify].

### H34 — Caspian shipping JV: shallow-draft container feeders, ro-ro and grain bulkers
- **COMBINES:** Middle Corridor growth (4.5 Mt; 300k TEU target by 2029) + an ageing Kazakh fleet (263 vessels, >70% older than 25 years) + barley exports to Iran lost to a vessel shortage + falling Caspian water levels needing new low-draft designs.

Fleet facts and the "Our cargo – our port – our fleet" policy ([Times of CA](https://timesca.com/development-of-a-maritime-fleet-in-a-landlocked-country/)). Vessel shortage hit Iran barley shipments ([Interfax](https://interfax.com/newsroom/top-stories/115880/)).
- **VALUE-CHAIN GAP:** ASCO (Azerbaijan) carries most Aktau/Kuryk–Baku traffic. Kazakhstan captures little freight margin. The KMG–AD Ports fleet JV "remains on paper".
- **SPECIFIC INVESTMENT OPPORTUNITY:** A ship-owning and operating JV with a global or Gulf operator (AD Ports, DP World, MSC/Medlog, a Turkish owner). Phase 1: charter-in plus 6–10 newbuild shallow-draft feeders and ro-ro vessels. Phase 2: grain bulkers for Iran and Russia's Volga ports. Optional: a repair yard at Kuryk.
- **OUTPUT:** Caspian container, ro-ro and bulk shipping services.
- **BUYER:** Freight forwarders, rail operators (KTZ, ADY), grain traders, Chinese shippers.
- **REACHABLE MARKET:** The Trans-Caspian freight market (11 Mt target by 2030 per the same source).
- **WHY KAZAKHSTAN:** Cargo origin (China transit + own grain and metals), state policy pushing for a national fleet, and port JVs already in place.
- **STRONGEST ALTERNATIVE LOCATION:** Azerbaijan/ASCO expanding its own fleet (modernisation to 2029); Turkmenistan (Turkmenbashi port).
- **INVESTOR RETURN LOGIC:** Freight rates on a capacity-constrained route; long-term charters with KTZ or forwarders.
- **MAIN OBSTACLE:** Caspian vessels must be built in-region or transit Volga–Don canals (size limits). Cargo volatility. Russia/Iran sanctions complexity.
- **WHY THIS COULD BE NON-OBVIOUS:** A landlocked country investing in ships.
- **NEXT DECISIVE TEST:** Get Trans-Caspian freight rates and vessel utilisation (ASCO, KMTF), plus newbuild options and costs (Baku shipyard, Volga yards, Kuryk).

### H35 — Border grain-to-container transloading and silo terminal, using Chinese "empties" for backhaul
- **COMBINES:** grain and oilseed exports to China + congestion at Dostyk/Altynkol + a huge China→West container flow that returns partly empty + gauge break (1520/1435).
- **INPUT / EXISTING CAPABILITY:** Khorgos Gateway (>372k TEU in 2025, with an existing wheat transshipment facility) ([Astana Times](https://astanatimes.com/2026/02/khorgos-gateway-boosts-transit-capacity-handles-over-372000-teus-in-2025/)). Barley to China fell 35% on border-station congestion ([Interfax](https://interfax.com/newsroom/top-stories/115880/)).
- **VALUE-CHAIN GAP:** Bulk grain hoppers queue at the gauge change. Containerised grain in returning boxes is under-used [verify empty-return share].
- **SPECIFIC INVESTMENT OPPORTUNITY:** A grain/oilseed/pulse silo-and-stuffing terminal on the KZ side (inland at a 1520 hub, or at the border), contracting with Chinese container lines for backhaul. Partner: COFCO, DP World (which already has Khorgos ties [verify]) or COSCO Shipping.
- **OUTPUT:** Containerised grain, flax and pulses into China; storage services.
- **BUYER:** Chinese importers and feed/oil processors; Kazakh exporters.
- **REACHABLE MARKET:** KZ→China agri trade (~US$1bn+ in 2023 per [Times of CA](https://timesca.com/kazakhstan-to-export-meat-and-live-cattle-to-china/)).

- **STRONGEST ALTERNATIVE LOCATION:** Russia (grain to China via Zabaikalsk); improvements on the Chinese side of the border.
- **INVESTOR RETURN LOGIC:** Throughput fees plus trading margin from lower logistics cost; a strategic asset for a Chinese grain buyer.
- **MAIN OBSTACLE:** Chinese phytosanitary and quota rules (wheat TRQ). Who controls container allocation.
- **WHY THIS COULD BE NON-OBVIOUS:** It solves two problems with one asset: empty containers and congested hoppers.
- **NEXT DECISIVE TEST:** Get the empty-container share on the westbound→eastbound return and Chinese rules for containerised grain imports via Khorgos.
