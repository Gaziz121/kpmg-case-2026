- For the 13 OPs below, this status supersedes the screening status (all were UWT). The screening files and `master_universe_2026-10-23.csv` are unchanged.
- The 13 validation reports and the 23-candidate independent review are **not in the tracked repository**; this table is the repository record of their outcome.
- Reopen conditions are reviewer-proposed from the recorded reason; the team may amend them. A reopened thesis still passes the opening gate in `DECISIONS.md` (24.10).
