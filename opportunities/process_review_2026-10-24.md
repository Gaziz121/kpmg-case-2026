## A. Overall verdict

**PROCESS IS MOSTLY SUFFICIENT — ONE SMALL FIX THEN PRIORITIZE.**

The one small fix is to finish the Argon handoff: apply the existing cheap screen to OP-102–104 and settle the recorded admission/boundary questions in the same short handoff. Then close discovery and allocate validation effort. This is completion of the existing process, not another audit or methodology phase.

The process is sufficient to choose what deserves research. It has not yet demonstrated five defensible investment recommendations. Those are different standards. The missing work is predominantly positive validation of reachable demand, the actual investment opening and Kazakhstan's comparative economics.

The official case permits justified assumptions and a freely chosen method. It requires a reasoned longlist/shortlist, five analyses and one concise teaser by 26 October. It does not require an exhaustive enumeration of Kazakhstan's economy. Preserving time to substantiate and communicate five cases now has greater expected value than improving discovery completeness further. That is a resource-allocation judgment, not a claim that further search would find nothing.

### How much independence and coverage can actually be claimed?

Do not describe the original four streams as four independent replications of the opportunity universe. Of the original 132 source rows, **128 came from the GZ and AB consolidations**; BK supplied three and SG one. GZ normalizes the old 39-card pass. AB discloses its assistant's previous exposure to that pass and inherited H-based material. Splitting bundled records and rediscovering the same idea do not create independent evidence.

The clean searches and Argon recovery improve confidence because they use different starting questions. Their limitations still matter: SG reports English/Russian news-led coverage, lightly examined structured sources and a bias toward investment announcements made after location choice. BK did not directly inspect the main procurement/privatization portals. Some clean-search rejections also use incumbency/capture language more broadly than the later careful screening does. A zero-card route therefore cannot certify that a whole opportunity class has no remaining openings.

These limitations warrant honest coverage claims, not another sprint. The existing universe already contains relevant service, procurement-linked and brownfield hypotheses that can be validated. I found no demonstrated missing search route important enough to displace that work. Service/business-model coverage is thinner than industrial localization coverage, but thin coverage is not proof that the final five require another route.

## C. Discovery stopping decision

**Should we continue searching for new opportunities after Argon reconciliation? NO.**

The stopping case rests on coverage of different investment mechanisms and the remaining delivery burden, not on 104 being a magic number. Argon added three distinct hypotheses: it demonstrates that additional search can still generate novelty. It does not demonstrate that those additions are superior investments, and structural QA establishes usability rather than commercial validity.

The admitted records already create far more consequential tests than the team can responsibly fund before submission. Another broad sweep could increase choice while reducing the evidence behind the eventual choices. Close discovery after the small handoff described above. Retain the locked method's exception only if later validation exposes a specific materially different decision, with a bounded test and an explicit statement of which existing work it displaces. Do not schedule that exception as another planned search.

## D. Screening interpretation

**Preserve the results and move forward. Do not rerun the 101-row screen or recalibrate statuses to manufacture PASSes.**

- **0 PASS:** no record cleared the applied standard. Central gaps remain in investment access, demand, qualification, comparative cost or returns. This is a diagnostic of the present evidence, not a measurement of the underlying opportunity success rate.
- **68 UWT:** a queue of potentially useful tests. It is not 68 validated opportunities, 68 equally attractive opportunities, or an approved workload.
- **31 PARK:** a defensible choice not to fund the current proposition now, often because differentiation, timing or scope is weak. They are not all proven bad investments and need not all be researched to justify exclusion.
- **2 FAIL:** narrowly framed mechanisms considered contradicted by evidence. The count is naturally small under a rule that missing information is not FAIL. It is not evidence that the remaining 99 are commercially sound.

There is visible calibration coherence: all batches preserve unresolved commercial hinges and distinguish incumbency from proof that an entire market is closed. Some UWT/PARK boundaries are debatable, as the notes acknowledge. Handle only consequential boundary calls during prioritization. In particular, OP-017's narrow mechanism and direct legal-text limitation need human confirmation before adopting that FAIL; OP-076's FAIL must remain confined to the identified route-build scope. This review does not independently verify either external claim.

The applied PASS bar is more demanding than merely surviving a first contradiction search. That explains some of the zero count. Do not turn a competition case into transaction due diligence: actual signed offtakes, private contract access and final engineering quotations are not universal competition requirements. Use sourced analogues, independent calculations and justified ranges where permitted. A central conclusion must survive those ranges; unsupported optimism does not close a decisive UWT. Residual ordinary uncertainty can remain disclosed.

## E. Next steps, in order

1. **Close the existing handoff today.** Cheap-screen only OP-102–104 under the same standard. Confirm the Argon admission/boundary calls in the same session. Preserve the earlier screening history and record which recommendations humans actually adopt. Do not build another QA layer.
2. **Immediately fund a small first wave.** In one team discussion, select the cases worth testing next on plausible economics, accessible evidence, time to resolve and overlap. A workload of roughly two cases per person is a useful starting budget, not a mandatory shortlist size. Give each a named owner, a different challenger and its existing decisive question. Leave unfunded UWTs unfunded without inventing evidence of failure.
3. **Use 24 October for decisive validation.** Test the investment opening, residual reachable demand/minimum viable scale and the factor on which Kazakhstan must beat the actual alternative. Domestic incumbents, imports and existing capacity may be the relevant alternatives. Verify decisive sources; use justified ranges and a break-even calculation. If a non-public response is unavailable, use an honest supported proxy where defensible or park the case. Apply the existing two-strike rule instead of extending research indefinitely.
4. **Make the human selection after that evidence, targeting 24 October.** Review the strongest NO case, location-choice/outcome comparables where obtainable, specific agency action and the weakest assumption for serious contenders. Do these checks inside the same opportunity note, not in new audit workstreams. Resolve relevant package overlap and retain a small reserve if practical. The selection rationale must show affirmative evidence for inclusion, not just absence of a fatal flaw.
5. **Protect 25 October for production and 26 October for QA/submission.** Produce the five analyses, one teaser, Excel longlist/shortlist and one methodology slide. Reconcile numbers, assumptions and working source links across them. The official deadline is 26 October; the PDF gives no exact hour. This review does not confirm a later cutoff or justify leaving submission to the last moment.

These are recommendations for the team; no cases are ranked or selected by this review.

## F. What not to do

- No more blind discovery rounds, generic coverage audits, database dumps, industrial-map sweeps or HS/OKED rankings for completeness.
- No repeat normalization or reconciliation of all records; address only boundaries that affect current decisions.
- No re-screening all 101 because the PASS column is empty, and no arbitrary PASS target.
- No equal research allocation across the 68 UWTs, no universal primary-source rehabilitation of every parked hypothesis, and no deep comparables/base-rate exercise for every row.
- No weighted-score framework, new thresholds or new governance architecture to postpone a human allocation decision.
- No compulsory private-contract or signed-offtake standard beyond what is needed to support the competition claim with honest evidence and assumptions.
- No further isolation/provenance reviews without concrete evidence of substantive contamination; retain the existing source trail.
- No polishing unselected hypotheses or spending 25 October improving methodology prose.

Keep the controls that protect the final recommendation: verify decisive claims, compare the real alternative, challenge the economics, expose assumptions and preserve the selection/exclusion trail.

## G. Final challenge: the single biggest risk

**UWT survival being mistaken for positive evidence of an incremental, competitive investment opening.**

The files make this specific. OP-030 identifies an awarded anchor grinding-ball contract and asks whether enough residual demand remains. OP-063 identifies existing authorised domestic gas-turbine repair capacity and asks what workload remains outside it and OEM agreements. OP-098 corrects a foreign-only repair comparison by identifying domestic workshops, but still needs evidence of a profitable unserved work basket. These examples are not recommendations or a ranking. They show why a plausible local-proximity story can survive cheap screening while its incremental investment case remains unproven.

The funding risk is measurable. The 68 UWT test descriptions specify **3,885 minutes, or 64.75 person-hours**, before deeper validation, challenger review or production. Calculation: 18 × 45 + 47 × 60 + 1 × 75 + 2 × 90 minutes. These are proposed allowances, not actual time spent, and some tests depend on unavailable buyer, vendor or owner information. Owners are generally proposed roles, “Codex,” or still to be assigned. The files do not establish accepted human ownership of the whole queue or completed validation elsewhere.

If the team keeps all those options alive, the deadline can force selection by familiarity, document polish or an appealing announcement. Prevent that by choosing a small funded wave now and requiring positive evidence of reachable business and comparative economics before final inclusion.

**The current plan is good enough if its next action is selective validation. A plan that waits for completeness, more PASS labels or another audit is wrong for the remaining time. Stop improving the process and start making investment decisions.**
