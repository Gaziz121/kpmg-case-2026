Structured records are leads and external facts, not investment recommendations. Keep this work within the existing practical discovery guidance below. No giant database scans, commercial subscription acquisition, scrapers/data pipelines or exhaustive OKED/HS-code rankings; data access must not become a side project.

Complete both assigned routes, verify the strongest leads with useful sources, prune vague leads and write cards. Approximately **60–90 minutes total per person** is practical guidance, not a hard clock or a synchronization requirement. Limit scope, not collaboration timing. Keep both routes isolated from prior lists until the owner's independent block is complete.

- Up to two usable distinct hypotheses per route; no minimum; at most four per person. A route may return zero. Include a brief route-completion note even when it returns no cards.
- Roughly 150–200 words per card, excluding links and field names. Show the causal chain: **external fact → investor/customer problem → capital decision → Kazakhstan hypothesis**. A polished narrative without an evidenced external anchor is insufficient.
- Provide initial source links supporting specific claims; prefer company annual reports, investor presentations, capex announcements, procurement plans, operator materials, project disclosures, regulatory/trade data and relevant industry associations. For every submitted card, the human researcher opens and inspects the central cited source(s) and confirms support for the attributed claims. No mandatory source count or citation-count quality score. Do not pad citations or fill missing evidence with AI inference; record the gap as `not established`.
- Label substantive statements FACT / CALCULATION / ASSUMPTION / INFERENCE / JUDGMENT. Unknown fields remain `not established` or `unknown`.
- Before freezing each submitted card, make one focused attempt to disprove or materially weaken its central inference: for example, an opening already captured/unavailable, no real investor/customer problem, a capital decision that is not investable, an obvious rival that dominates, or an unsupported Kazakhstan-specific causal link. Use a fresh AI context/model when the central inference is consequential or uncertain, under the same clean-room restrictions; do not require routine second-model verification of straightforward facts or duplicate research. Record a short note by working ID: supported claims; overclaims/corrections; important contradiction if found; remaining decisive uncertainty. This is discovery verification, not full investment validation.
- No ranking, stars, Top 5, detailed DCF/IRR, long sector reports or promotional language. Do not solve the whole case.
- Stop a route when it has been meaningfully explored, useful candidates have external evidence anchors, obvious contrary explanations have received a targeted check and important gaps are disclosed. Further searches increasingly repeat known actors, documents or mechanisms, and more open-ended searching is unlikely to improve the screening universe enough to justify delaying validation and production. Keep at most two usable distinct hypotheses per route; zero or one is acceptable, and weak ideas must not fill quotas. Stop pursuing a lead if it remains only a sector/trend, lacks an articulable investor decision or paying market, relies on a generic Kazakhstan advantage, or encounters an evidenced fatal contradiction. Record an evidence-backed rejection without turning missing evidence into FAIL.
- Freeze the discovery output after both bounded routes and source/card review are complete. Do not expand a stream into several hours of open-ended searching because a model offers more ideas.
